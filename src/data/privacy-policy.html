Privacy Policy — UT Compass

Last updated 13 August 2026

UT Compass does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. The app has no backend and no analytics software. We receive nothing about you or how you use it.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following in your device's own storage: the entries you create (title, room, day, time), the categories you create or edit, your appearance preference, and a flag noting you've seen the welcome screen. This never leaves your device unless you export it yourself. It is removed when you delete the app, or through Settings, then Clear All Data.

Location

If you grant location permission, it is used only to show your position on the campus map while you are looking at it. It is not recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. Permission is optional and the app works fully without it. You can grant or revoke it in your device's Settings.

Exporting your data

You can export your entries as a file to back them up. You choose where that file goes using your device's sharing tools. We never receive it. Once you send a backup somewhere, its privacy depends on wherever you sent it.

Opening other apps

When you tap for directions, the app passes the destination's coordinates and name to whichever maps app you pick. That app's own privacy policy governs what happens next. Nothing about you is included. The contact option opens your email app with a draft; nothing is sent unless you send it.

Campus directory

The list of buildings, food, study spots, and landmarks is bundled into the app at install. Browsing it makes no network request and reveals nothing about what you viewed.

Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third-party services

No advertising networks, no analytics providers, no third-party tracking software.

Changes

If a future version changes what the app stores or accesses, this page will be updated before that version is released and the date above will change.
